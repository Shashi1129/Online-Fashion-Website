Privacy & Policy

Your privacy is our priority

BRANDALE is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, and use the information you provide when using our websites, apps, stores, or social media pages.

1. Personal Information We Collect

  • Identification & contact info: name, address, phone, email.
  • Payment information: credit card, bank account, PayPal details.
  • Demographic info: gender, birthday.
  • Images & voice: store visits (CCTV), calls to client service.
  • Preferences & interests: disclosed during interactions with client advisors.
  • Health-related info (cosmetic products).
  • Public posts or social media submissions.
  • Purchase details online or in-store.
  • Browser and browsing behavior on our websites.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To improve products, services, and online experience.
  • To process orders, answer requests, and manage complaints.
  • For security purposes against fraud or unauthorized activities.
  • To manage possible adverse effects of cosmetic products.
  • For marketing purposes (with consent).

3. Sharing Your Information

  • Parent and affiliated companies for internal purposes.
  • Service providers performing services on our behalf.
  • Banks and online payment services.
  • Business partners (with consent).
  • Third parties in case of business restructuring.

4. Cookies and Tracking Tools

  • Functional cookies: language, login, shopping basket.
  • Behavioral cookies: browsing, referring URLs, page access.
  • Analytics: website activity and content visibility improvement.
  • Security: device fingerprinting for online transactions.

5. Transferring Your Information

Your personal information may be transferred and processed in countries where BRANDALE operates to provide better services.

6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or data portability of your personal information. You may also provide instructions on data processing after your death.

7. Protecting Your Information

We use technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, but please note that internet security cannot be guaranteed. Exercise caution when accessing our services online.
